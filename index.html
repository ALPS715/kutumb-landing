Your neighbourhood, simplified.

The problem

Every society runs. Few run smoothly.

Right now, your community probably lives across a WhatsApp group, a treasurer's spreadsheet, and someone's memory. It works — until it doesn't.

Dues

Collection by follow-up

Chasing 40+ flats every month for maintenance, one “please confirm 🙏” at a time — with no clean record of who paid what, when.

Money

Reconciled by hand

The treasurer tallies UPI screenshots against an Excel sheet at month-end. One missed entry, and the corpus figure is a debate at the next AGM.

Everything else

Buried in the group chat

Notices scroll away under good-morning forwards. Complaints have no owner, no status. Documents live in someone's Drive.

One app, both sides of the notice board

Made for residents. Made for the committee.

The same app, two very different jobs — and it does both without a manual.

For residents

Stay informed. Pay in one tap.

  • Pay maintenance with UPI in seconds — an instant receipt lands against your flat, every time.
  • Raise maintenance tickets and watch them move — raised, assigned, resolved. No more “what happened to my complaint?”
  • Read every notice in one feed — water shutdowns, AGM dates, parking rules — archived, never scrolled away.
  • Vote in polls on real decisions, with results everyone can see.
  • Emergency contacts and society documents — bylaws, receipts, NOCs — a tap away, downloadable any time.
For the committee — President, Treasurer, Secretary

Run the society. Not the paperwork.

  • Broadcast notices to every flat in one send — no forwarding chains, no “who saw it?”
  • Manage dues and late fees — raise monthly demands by BHK or area, and see paid vs. pending at a glance.
  • Approve and track expenses with a clean trail — every rupee out is recorded against who approved it.
  • Watch the corpus fund and ledger update themselves as money moves — collections in, expenses out, balance live.
  • Manage residents and billing setup — flats, owners, tenants, rates — from one screen, not one register.

How it works

From download to live in an afternoon.

01

Get the Android app

We send your committee a direct install link (APK) as part of early access — it takes a couple of minutes to set up on any Android phone.

02

Register your society

Name your society, add blocks and flats, set maintenance rates by BHK or area. You're the admin — add more committee members whenever you like.

03

Share the invite code

Drop your society's invite code in the WhatsApp group you already have. Residents join with a phone number and OTP — and the first dues cycle can go out the same day.

Android only, for now. EzyHood is in early access and ships as a direct Android install — there's no app-store listing yet, and that's by design while we work closely with our first societies.

Questions committees ask us

The honest FAQ.

Which phones does EzyHood work on?

Android — that's it for now. An iOS app is on the roadmap, and early-access societies will be the first to know when it's coming. If a few residents are on iPhone today, the committee can record their payments and share notices on their behalf in the meantime.

Is it on the Play Store?

Not yet. During early access we send your committee a direct Android install link (APK) and walk you through installing it — it takes a couple of minutes. A store listing is planned as we open up beyond the first cohort.

What does early access cost?

Nothing. Early-access societies use EzyHood free while we build with you. When paid plans arrive, founding societies get first — and best — terms, and we'll tell you well in advance.

How do residents join?

Your society gets an invite code. Drop it in the WhatsApp group you already have; residents install the app and join with their phone number and an OTP. No passwords, no forms, no per-resident setup for the committee.

Can maintenance be billed by flat size?

Yes — set rates per BHK type or per square foot, plus late fees. EzyHood raises the monthly demand for every flat automatically and tracks paid vs. pending for you.

Who can be an admin?

EzyHood starts with one admin role, and you can extend it to as many committee members as you need — President, Treasurer, Secretary, or anyone else you trust. No permission hierarchy to configure first.

What's next on the roadmap?

With our first societies we're refining dues, ledger and notices. Further out we're exploring assistive features — things like notice summaries, smarter due reminders and automatic ticket categorisation. None of that is live today, and we won't market it until it is.
