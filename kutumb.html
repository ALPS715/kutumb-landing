Features

Everything your society needs

One app. Every committee headache — solved.

Maintenance Dues
Generate and send dues automatically. Residents pay with UPI in one tap. Track who's paid, who hasn't — no more awkward follow-up calls.
Help Desk Tickets
Residents raise complaints directly. Admins assign, track, and close them. Every issue has a thread, a status, and an owner.
Notices & Announcements
Push a notice to every resident in seconds. Water shutdown, AGM date, parking rules — all neatly archived, never lost in a group chat.
Expense Approvals
Multi-role approval workflows with a full audit trail. From plumber bill to lift AMC — every rupee spent is documented and approved.
Document Vault
Store bylaws, NOCs, maintenance receipts, and society documents securely. Find anything in seconds, share with the right people only.
Polls & Voting
Run AGM votes, decide on vendor selection, or just pick the Diwali celebration date — all transparent, all recorded, no disputes.
Setup

Up and running in minutes

No IT team needed. No lengthy onboarding calls. If you can use WhatsApp, you can set up Kutumb.

1
Admin Sets Up
Create your society, add your buildings, floors, and flat numbers. Takes about two minutes from phone to live.
2
Residents Join
Share a 6-digit invite code. Residents sign up with just their phone number — OTP verified, no passwords to remember.
3
Everything Flows
Dues get generated, tickets get raised, notices go out — all in one place. Your committee actually has time to breathe.
